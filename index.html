Will vs. Going To

Form & Uses

متى نستخدم will ومتى نستخدم going to؟

When was the decision made?

Before speaking

“I’ve already booked the tickets — I am going to visit AlUla.”

going to · plan
At the moment of speaking

“Your bag looks heavy! I will help you.”

will · instant decision
The future

The action happens later — both forms talk about the future.

WLL

Will

⚡

We use will for:

  1. 1

    A decision made at the moment.

    قرار يُتَّخذ في لحظة الكلام

  2. 2

    A prediction about the future.

    توقّع عن المستقبل

Form
Subject+ will+ base verb
+Affirmative I will help you.
−Negative I won’t help you. (won’t = will not)
?Question Will you help me?
GATE NOWCLASS DECIDE · PREDICT
VS
GNT

Going To

🗓️

We use going to for:

  1. 1

    A plan or intention made before speaking.

    خطة أو نية مُقرَّرة قبل الكلام

  2. 2

    Something that is likely to happen (based on evidence).

    شيء مُرجَّح حدوثه بناءً على دليل

Form
Subject+ am / is / are+ going to+ base verb
+Affirmative I am going to visit AlUla.
−Negative I’m not going to visit AlUla.
?Question Are you going to visit AlUla?
GATE PLANNEDCLASS PLAN · EVIDENCE

Two kinds of future guesses

💭

Prediction (opinion / belief)

“I think it will be sunny in AlUla tomorrow.”

will
🌩️

Likely to happen (evidence you can see)

“Look at those dark clouds! It’s going to rain.”

going to
★

Remember!

  • Use will for decisions and predictions.
  • Use going to for plans and things that are likely to happen (evidence).
